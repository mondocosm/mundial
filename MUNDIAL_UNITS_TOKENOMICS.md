# Mundial Units (MU) - Tokenomics V0.1

## 1. Overview

Mundial Units (Symbol: MU - represented graphically as a square-cornered 'U' nested inside a square-cornered 'M' without touching, forming a square bounding box) are the native, feeless cryptocurrency of the Mondocosm platform. Designed for fast, efficient micro-transactions within the ecosystem, MU facilitates value exchange for data access, computational resources, asset trading, and participation rewards.

## 2. Core Principles (Nano/Banano Inspired)

*   **Feeless Transactions:** Transactions on the MU network do not incur direct network fees, encouraging high-frequency use and micro-payments.
*   **Fast Confirmation:** Leveraging a block-lattice architecture (similar to Nano), transactions are designed to confirm near-instantly. Each account manages its own blockchain, and transactions only require updates to the sender's and receiver's chains.
*   **Scalability:** The block-lattice design aims for high throughput, avoiding the bottlenecks associated with traditional single-blockchain systems.
*   **Eco-Friendly:** The consensus mechanism (likely Open Representative Voting - ORV, similar to Nano) is lightweight and energy-efficient compared to Proof-of-Work.

## 3. Symbol & Branding

The symbol graphically represents the integration within the Mondocosm ('M') platform, focusing on the User ('U'). The square, non-touching design evokes modularity and structure.

## 4. Utility within Mondocosm

Mundial Units will serve multiple purposes:

*   **Resource Access:** Paying for access to specific datasets, computational resources (e.g., voxelization, NeRF rendering), or advanced platform features.
*   **Asset Trading:** Buying, selling, or licensing geospatial assets, scenes, worlds, or NFTs created within Mondocosm.
*   **Staking/Delegation (Potential):** Users might delegate their voting weight to representatives running nodes, potentially earning rewards.
*   **Rewards:** Distributing rewards for contributions to the ecosystem (e.g., data validation, open-source development, community support).
*   **Governance (Potential):** Potentially used for voting on platform upgrades or parameter changes.

*   **Fork:** Based on a fork of Nano or a similar implementation like Banano.
*   **Network:** Requires a network of representative nodes run by the community or foundation to validate transactions via ORV.
*   **Wallet Integration:** Requires wallet integration within the Mundial frontend and potentially standalone wallets.

## 7. Relation to IOTA/Shimmer

While MU serves as the primary feeless transaction currency, IOTA and Shimmer will be utilized for functionalities requiring smart contracts, complex data transactions, or tokenization (NFTs) where their specific features (DAG structure, native assets, smart contracts) are more suitable. MU and IOTA/Shimmer assets may be bridged or swapped via integrated mechanisms or decentralized exchanges.
