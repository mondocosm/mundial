# Mondocosm Elevator Pitch

Mondocosm is building a decentralized metaverse focused on our world. It's a gamified web platform where users can collaboratively capture, create, edit, and share version-controlled 3D geospatial data using intuitive tools like node-based workflows and specialized editors.

Powered by Kart for Git-like geospatial versioning and a unique gameplay loop where content popularity drives visibility, Mondocosm fosters community curation. It integrates diverse data formats (from point clouds to NeRFs), multiple visualization engines (maps, globes, VR), avatar systems, and its own feeless cryptocurrency, "Mundial Units," creating an open, extensible, and engaging geospatial ecosystem.
